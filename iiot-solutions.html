Industrial IoT (IIoT) & Remote Monitoring

Live data from your tanks, pumps and analyzers — on a dashboard, with alarms when something needs attention.

Field to Dashboard

How your data reaches you

Sense

Your field instruments: 4-20 mA or Modbus outputs.

Node

A local device reads the signal at site and prepares it for transmission.

Connect

GSM/4G, LoRaWAN, UHF radio or Ethernet carries it through a gateway to the cloud.

Dashboard

SCADA or cloud dashboard, on desktop or mobile, with SMS/email alarms when a value goes out of limit.

Retrofit First

Already have instruments? We connect them.

If your site already has working 4-20 mA or Modbus instruments, there is no need to replace them. We add a gateway alongside the existing loops and bring the data online without touching your control system.

Frequently Asked Questions

IIoT & remote monitoring questions we hear most

What is Industrial IoT (IIoT) remote monitoring?

It's a way to see your field instrument data — tank levels, flow, water quality, pump status, energy use — on a dashboard from anywhere, by connecting a gateway to your existing instruments.

Do I need to replace my existing instruments or control system?

No. We add a gateway alongside your existing 4-20 mA or Modbus loops and bring the data online without touching your control system.

What connectivity options do you support?

GSM/4G, LoRaWAN, UHF radio and Ethernet/Wi-Fi, depending on network coverage and site conditions.

Is the data secure?

Yes. Connections are encrypted and dashboard access is password-protected, with a read-only setup that keeps monitoring separate from your control system.

Can we start with just one tank or station?

Yes. Most clients begin with a single site or asset, then add more sites to the same dashboard as needed.

What happens if the network connection drops?

Data is buffered at the gateway and sent through once the network comes back, so you don't lose readings during an outage.

What do you need to see remotely?

Tell us what you want to monitor and where the sites are — we'll propose the right setup.
