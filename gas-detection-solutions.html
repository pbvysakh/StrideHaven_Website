Core Detection Categories

Continuous protection from point of hazard to control room

Fixed Gas Detectors

Fixed-point transmitters for continuous monitoring of toxic, combustible and oxygen levels at critical process and plant locations.

Portable Gas Detectors

Personal and area monitors for confined space entry, hot work permits, maintenance and turnaround activity.

Flame Detectors

UV, IR and multi-spectrum flame detectors for hydrocarbon fire risk areas and process units.

Gas Detection Controllers & Panels

Central controllers, alarm panels and relay logic that tie field detectors into your ESD / SIS architecture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Gas & flame detection questions we hear most

What gas detection equipment do you supply?

Fixed and portable gas detectors, flame detectors, and gas detection controllers and panels.

Are your gas detectors certified for hazardous areas?

Yes. We specify and integrate detectors certified to ATEX, IECEx and SIL requirements as required by the hazardous-area classification of your project.

Do you support confined space monitoring?

Yes. We provide portable monitor deployment and permit-to-work support for confined space and hot work activity.

Specifying gas or flame detection for a hazardous area?

Share your area classification and gas hazards and our engineering team will design the detection and alarm strategy for your project.
