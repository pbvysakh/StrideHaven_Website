Frequently Asked Questions

What clients ask us before they start a project

What does Stride Haven Engineering Solutions do?

We engineer, integrate, manufacture and deliver instrumentation, analytical, automation, gas detection, IoT and engineering solutions for process industries across the UAE, Middle East and Africa.

Which industries do you serve?

We work across oil and gas, petrochemical, power generation, water and wastewater treatment, desalination, utilities, and food and beverage industries.

Are you vendor-neutral?

Yes. We specify and integrate technology from leading global brands based on what fits your project, not a fixed catalogue — see our Technology Partners page for the brands we work with.

Do you provide engineering documentation for EPC projects?

Yes. We deliver FDS, datasheets, hook-up drawings, GAD, I/O lists, ITP, FAT/SAT and technical compliance documentation for EPC and consultant projects.

Can you manufacture custom panels and skids?

Yes. We manufacture analyzer panels, instrument panels and skid-mounted systems in-house in Sharjah, UAE, with FAT and inspection support.

Do you offer gas and flame detection solutions?

Yes. We supply fixed and portable gas detectors, flame detectors and confined space monitoring, engineered to ATEX, IECEx and SIL requirements.

How do I request a quote?

Send us your process conditions, RFQ or requirement through our Contact page, WhatsApp or email, and our engineering team will follow up with next steps.

Where is Stride Haven based?

We are based in Hamriya Free Zone, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, serving clients across the UAE, Middle East and Africa.
