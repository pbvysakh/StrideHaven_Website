Frequently Asked Questions

Analyzer questions we hear most

Which analyzer parameters can Stride Haven supply?

H2S, Hydrocarbon(HC), Ammonia(NH3), turbidity, chlorine, Total Organic Carbon (TOC), Total nitrogen (TN), Total phosphorus (TP), Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD), Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD), Oil in Water, silica(SiO2) and SDI analyzers for water treatment and process quality monitoring.

Do you supply sampling and conditioning systems?

Yes. We supply sampling and conditioning systems that deliver a stable, representative sample to the analyzer.

Are the analyzers you supply sourced through authorized channels?

Yes. Every analyzer we integrate is sourced through authorized channels, with full traceability back to the manufacturer.

Can analyzer outputs integrate with our existing DCS, SCADA or PLC?

Yes. We supply analyzers with standard 4-20mA, HART and Modbus outputs for seamless integration with your existing control and monitoring systems.

How often do analyzers need calibration?

Calibration frequency depends on the analyzer type and application. We offer scheduled calibration and verification support to keep readings accurate and compliant.

Do you design and build analyzer panels and shelters?

Yes. We design and fabricate analyzer panels and shelters matched to your sampling system, site conditions and area classification.

Do you supply auto sampling and multiplexing for multiple samples?

Yes. We supply auto samplers and stream multiplexing systems that allow a single analyzer to monitor multiple sample points in sequence.

Monitoring a water or process quality parameter?

Tell us what you need to measure and we'll recommend an analyzer and sampling setup.
